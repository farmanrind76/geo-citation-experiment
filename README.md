# GEO Citation Experiment

A public, 14-day log of how [farmanrind.com](https://farmanrind.com) earns citations in AI answers. Everything I change and every result I record goes in this repo, so you can check the work.

Method: my [GEO Playbook](https://github.com/farmanrind76/geo-playbook). Author: [Farman Rind](https://github.com/farmanrind76).

## Goal

Get farmanrind.com cited or named by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Gemini for a fixed set of prompts, starting from a recorded baseline.

## Dates

- Start: 15 November 2026
- End: 29 November 2026

## Fixed prompt set

The same prompts are checked on every surface at each check-in, logged-out where possible:

1. Who is Farman Rind, the SEO and GEO specialist?
2. Who are good GEO specialists for hire?
3. How do I get my brand cited in ChatGPT answers?
4. What is the difference between SEO and GEO?
5. Which SEO specialists in Pakistan work remotely with UK and US teams?

## What I record

For each prompt and surface: was farmanrind.com cited, was the name mentioned, and which sources were cited instead. Results go in [LOG.md](LOG.md).

## What I change (all logged)

- On-site: answer-first passages, FAQ and Person schema, named authorship, internal links
- Off-site: LinkedIn posts, directory and profile consistency, GitHub playbook, helpful community answers with disclosure
- Technical: AI crawler access in robots.txt, sitemap, Bing indexing

## Rules

- No fake reviews, no fake accounts, no bought links or stars
- Negative or empty results get logged too
- Check-ins are manual and honest. If a surface gives a different answer on a repeat run, both are recorded
